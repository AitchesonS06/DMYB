Intro

I build things, find what's broken, and fix it, whether it's an app, a business plan, or a beat.

I'm Shamar Aitcheson, a Cybersecurity & Business Administration major at Northeastern University. I'm a developer on Lost & Hound, a campus lost-and-found app now on the iOS App Store, and I mentor student dev teams through Oasis.

Since 2021 I've produced music as MartoDaGeneral, with records that have gotten spins on Hot 97, and my beat channel Martoonnabeat has passed a million views on YouTube. I'm also a fellow in the Braven Accelerator and a member of ColorStack.
